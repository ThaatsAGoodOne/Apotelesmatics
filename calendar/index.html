How the calendars and time lords are reckoned

Gregorian and Julian dates are proleptic: each calendar's leap-year rule is carried back and forward without the 1582 switch, so any day can be read in either. Transit times are shown in this device's time zone.

Babylonian dates are reconstructed for Babylon by observation, not taken from historical tables. Each month begins on the evening the new crescent is first seen. The crescent must pass both Kolev's heliacal twilight test (the same one used for the planets' heliacal phases) and Yallop's q-test (NAO Technical Note 69, 1997), which allows for how thin the crescent is. Months have 29 or 30 days. The Babylonian day ran from sunset to sunset, so each calendar date is labelled with the Babylonian day whose daylight falls on it; day 1 began the evening before. The year begins with Nisannu, placed by MUL.APIN's Pleiades rule: if the Moon meets the Pleiades on the 1st of Nisannu the year is normal, and if only on the 3rd, the year is a leap year, so a second Addaru is added first. Because that rule follows the stars, not the equinox, Nisannu falls later in the solar year as the centuries pass (around March–April in antiquity, April–May now). Years are counted in the Seleucid Era (year 1 began with Nisannu 311 BC) and from Nabonassar (Nisannu 747 BC), both in Babylonian years.

Transits are the moments a moving planet perfects a conjunction, sextile, square, trine or opposition, by longitude, to a natal planet, the Ascendant or the Midheaven. A planet that stations near a point can perfect the same aspect up to three times.

Time lords: the profected Ascendant's sign and lord (the Lord of the Year); the distributor, the bound lord of the significator directed through the bounds; and the partner, the planet whose ray the directed significator reached most recently. The last two use the calculator's bound distribution (the chart's own bounds system, direct, with the chosen key).

Zodiacal Releasing runs from the Lots of Fortune and Spirit, as on the calculator page (Spirit moves on a sign when it shares Fortune's), showing when each level 1 and level 2 period begins, with any Loosing of the Bond (LB), foreshadowing (FS) or completion (CP). A Spirit period in a sign angular to Fortune's (the 1st, 4th, 7th or 10th sign from it) is a peak periodand is marked with its angle; the 10th from Fortune is the strongest.
